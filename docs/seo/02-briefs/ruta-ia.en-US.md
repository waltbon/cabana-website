## Estructura fija
1. **Client moment:** the meeting where someone asks "where do we start with AI?" and the answer is an opinion.
2. **Promise:** leadership knows which opportunities to start with, which the data supports today, what to fix for the rest. Término principal en el primer párrafo.
3. **What you get:** AI Opportunity Map (top 5, each *ready to start / needs remediation / not now*, with business decision, owner, blocker); executive report; remediation roadmap; readout session; follow-up calls at day 7 and 21.
4. **How it works:** week 1 opportunity workshop; weeks 2–3 interviews and direct review of your systems; week 4 assessment, map, readout. "From your people and your systems, not a generic benchmark."
5. **Duration and price:** 4 weeks + 30 days follow-up; USD 12,000 fixed (forma de impuesto pendiente, C5); 30/70; 50% credited to an implementation project started within 90 days.
6. **Who it's not for:** idea already chosen → AI First Move; decision+budget → direct project; known data gap → capability project; >2 business units or industrial OT.
7. **FAQ** (objeciones brief §3.6): "Do you deliver a business case?" → reformular sin el término: "Do you give us the financial projection?" No; … · "Our team can make the list." · "What if no idea is ready?" · "Why pay for an assessment before implementation?"
8. **CTA:** Book a conversation.

- "AI readiness" una vez en un H2/FAQ (variante); el pilar de readiness vive en el blog (checklist).
